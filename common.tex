% ---------- SIDE B: SCORING ----------
\newcommand{\sideB}{%
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Scoring}
  \textbf{Contracts made}\gap

  Points for contracts made are won by the declaring side. The declaring
  side scores points only if they win the target number of tricks, or more.\gap

  No points are won for the first 6 tricks. Every trick won thereafter,
  including any beyond the contract's target, scores points according to the
  contract as follows:\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}l@{}}
    \makebox[0.27\linewidth][l]{\Club\ or \Diamondsuit} & 20 points\\
    \makebox[0.27\linewidth][l]{\Heart\ or \Spade} & 30 points\\
    \makebox[0.27\linewidth][l]{No trumps} & 40 points for trick 7, 30 points thereafter
  \end{tabular}\gap

  Bonuses are awarded for the contract declared, not for extra tricks won:\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.45\linewidth}@{}>{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.20\linewidth}@{}}
    For a part-score contract: & 50 points\\
    (at least 7 tricks) &
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.45\linewidth}@{}>{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.20\linewidth}@{}}
    For a game contract: & 300 points\\
    \multicolumn{2}{@{}p{0.98\linewidth}@{}}{(at least 9 tricks in no trumps, 10 tricks in \Heart\ or \Spade,
      11 tricks in \Club\ or \Diamondsuit)}
  \end{tabular}\gap

  \textbf{Unsuccessful contracts}\gap

  If a contract is not made (i.e., the target number of tricks is not reached),
  the defending side scores points instead of the declaring side. The
  defenders score as follows, for each trick short of the target:\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.45\linewidth}@{}>{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{0.20\linewidth}@{}}
    Per undertrick: & 50 points
  \end{tabular}
  \vfill{\fontsize{5.5}{6.5}\selectfont\noindent\expandafter\url\expandafter{\footurl}}}

% ---------- SIDE A: RULES ----------
\newcommand{\sideA}{%
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Rules}
  \begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item The cards are shuffled and dealt, 13 each to the 4 players
      (or taken from a pre-dealt board).
    \item Points are counted and announced, starting with the dealer and
      taking turns clockwise, using the scale:\\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \hspace*{0.4em}Ace = 4\quad King = 3\quad Queen = 2\quad Jack = 1
    \item The points for each partnership are totalled and the side with the
      most points becomes the declaring side. The declarer is the member of the
      declaring side with the most points, or the first to announce if equal.
      Declarer's partner's hand is placed face up on the table as dummy.
    \item Declarer decides and announces the contract -- the target number of
      tricks and choice of trump suit or no trumps. Target numbers of tricks
      may be:\\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \hspace*{0.4em}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{\hspace{1.2em}}l@{}}
        Part score: 7+ & Game in no trumps: 9+\\
        Game in \Heart\ or \Spade: 10+ & Game in \Club\ or \Diamondsuit: 11+
      \end{tabular}
    \item The opening lead is made by the player on declarer's left. Tricks
      are played out clockwise, cards face up. Declarer decides the play of
      the cards from the dummy.
    \item After each trick has been completed, cards in that trick are turned
      face down. The winner of each trick leads the first card to the next trick.
    \item At the end of play, the tricks won and lost are counted and agreed,
      and the score is calculated.
    \item For the next hand, the dealer is the next player clockwise.
  \end{enumerate}}

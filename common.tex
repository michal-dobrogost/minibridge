% ---------- SIDE B: SCORING ----------
\newcommand{\sideB}{%
  \raggedright
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Scoring}

  Games are played to a predetermined goal like 1000 points or 8 deals.\gap

  \textbf{Contracts made}\gap

  Points for contracts made are won by the declaring side. The declaring
  side scores points only if they win the target number of tricks, or more.\gap

  No points are won for the first 6 tricks. Every trick won thereafter,
  including any beyond the contract's target, scores points according to the
  contract as follows:\gap

  \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}l@{}}
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Minor suit (\Club\ or \Diamondsuit)} & 20 points\\
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Major suit (\Heart\ or \Spade)} & 30 points\\
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{No trumps} & 40 points (trick 7), 30 points (tricks 8+)
  \end{tabular}\gap

  Bonuses are awarded for the contract announced:\gap


  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|}
    \hline
    Type & Trump suit & Number of tricks & Bonus \\
    \hline
    Game &
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
        Minor (\Club{} or \Diamondsuit)\\
        Major (\Heart{} or \Spade)\\
        No trump
      \end{tabular}} &
      {\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}\begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
        11 \\
        10 \\
        9
      \end{tabular}}
      & 300 points \\
    \hline
    %Part-score & \Club{}, \Diamondsuit{}, \Heart{}, \Spade{} or NT& 7 & 50 points \\
    %Part-score & --- & 7 & 50 points \\
    Part-score & any & 7 & 50 points \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \gap

  \textbf{Unsuccessful contracts}\gap

  If a contract is not made (i.e., the target number of tricks is not reached),
  the defending side scores points instead of the declaring side. The
  defenders score for every trick short of the target:

  \gap
  \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}l@{}}
    \makebox[0.4\linewidth][l]{Each undertrick} & 50 points\\
  \end{tabular}
  \vfill{\fontsize{5.5}{6.5}\selectfont\noindent\expandafter\url\expandafter{\footurl}}}

% ---------- SIDE A: RULES ----------
\newcommand{\sideA}{%
  \cardtitle{MiniBridge Rules}
  {\raggedright\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.5\baselineskip,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
    \item The cards are shuffled and dealt, 13 each to the 4 players.
    \item High card points (HCP) are summed and announced, clockwise, starting with the dealer:
      \\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \hspace*{0.4em}Ace = 4\quad King = 3\quad Queen = 2\quad Jack = 1
    \item
      Declarer's partner's hand is placed face up on the table as dummy.\\
      Partnership --- A team of two players sitting across from each other. \\
      Declaring side --- The partnership with the higher combined HCP. \\
      Declarer --- Player with the higher HCP or first to announce if equal.
    \item Declarer decides and announces the contract --- the target number of tricks (7+) and choice of trump suit or no trumps.
      Consider a trump contract if the declaring side has 8+ cards in a suit (a fit).
      The following table shows the number of tricks you may expect to make:
      \\[0.3\baselineskip]
      \begin{tabular}{|l|llll|}
        \hline
        \multirow{2}{*}{Contract suit} & \multicolumn{4}{c|}{Combined HCP} \\
        %\cline{2-5}
        & 21+ & 23+ & 25+ & 27+ \\
        \hline
        Minor (\Club{} or \Diamondsuit{}) & 8 & 9 & 10 & \textbf{11} \\
        Major (\Heart{} or \Spade{})      & 8 & 9 & \textbf{10} & -- \\
        No Trump                          & 7 & 8 & \textbf{9}  & -- \\
        \hline
      \end{tabular}
    \item The opening lead is made by the player left of declarer.
      Tricks are played out clockwise, cards face up.
      Declarer plays their own hand and the dummy.
      The highest card of the suit led wins the trick unless trumped.
      You must follow suit if you can.
    \item After each trick, the four cards are turned face down.
      The winner leads to the next trick.
    \item At the end of play, the tricks won are counted and scored.
    \item For the next hand, the dealer is the next player clockwise.
  \end{enumerate}}
}
